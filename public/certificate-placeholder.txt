To add the certificate image:
1. Open the PDF: Abubakri Faaruq Adebowale__N+_FIX.pdf
2. Take a screenshot of the certificate (or use Snipping Tool on Windows)
3. Save it as: CompTIA_Network+_Certificate.jpg or .png
4. Place it in this public folder
5. The website will automatically show the image on the certification card
